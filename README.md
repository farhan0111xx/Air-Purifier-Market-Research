
Air Purifier Market Research & Competitor Gap Analysis

 Project Overview
An end-to-end commercial research and product strategy project built in Microsoft Excel. This analysis combines regional air quality metrics (AQI), demographic potential, seasonal search demand, and competitor feature benchmarks to guide brand positioning in India.

---

 Executive Summary & Strategic Insights

 1. Competitor Benchmarking & Feature Gap Analysis
* Direct Comparison: Benchmarked market incumbents (e.g., Dyson GEN1 HP10 at ₹45,000) against our flagship specification (**Air Pure 6 Ultra** at ₹43,999).
* Competitive Advantage: Identified key feature gaps in competitor offerings, establishing our advantage through dual-vent air circulation, integrated app push notifications, and automatic sensitivity switching during pollen/AQI spikes.

2. Regional AQI & Environmental Metrics
* Pollution Distribution: Evaluated average and maximum AQI indices across key urban hubs to map demand intensity.
* City Prioritization: Categorized top 5 highest-risk cities needing immediate air purification solutions vs. bottom 5 cities to optimize regional supply chain and marketing spend.

 3. Tiered Market Entry Strategy
* Loni Ghaziabad :  Highest overall potential score (2.13) driven by extreme AQI levels and high population density—prime target for entry-level models.
* Delhi & Gurgaon :  Ranked 2nd and 3rd; high median household income makes them ideal hubs for high-margin, flagship purifiers.

 4. Sales Channels & Demand Seasonality

* Omni-Channel Demand: E-Commerce leads with 52% market share, supported by offline retail at **38%** for hands-on product interaction.
* Winter Seasonality: Search demand and purchase intent peak directly during Q4/Q1 winter AQI spikes (peaking up to 221 AQI).
* 
*  5. Product Costing & Bill of Materials (BOM)
    
* Supply Chain & Unit Economics: Built component-level Bills of Materials (BOM) across four distinct product models, detailing category breakdowns, component quantities, and INR unit costs to balance commercial viability.
